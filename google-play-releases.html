Chicken Flapper II and Zombie Block Now Live on Google Play

Two new MasterGamer releases are now available to download on Google Play.

Chicken Flapper II: Never Ending Journey launches players into a colourful vertical arcade adventure filled with enemy ships, golden eggs, power-ups, bosses and Hyperdrive bonus runs.

Zombie Block: Kill Everything delivers fast block-style zombie survival action as players fight through waves of undead enemies and battle to survive the chaos.

Both titles can now be downloaded from Google Play, with additional platform releases and MasterGamer updates to follow.
