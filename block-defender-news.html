Block Defender — Coming Soon

The invasion has begun. Your job is simple: hold the line.

Block Defender is a fast-paced arcade action game where you pilot your defender through increasingly intense waves of enemy fighters, gunships and massive airborne threats.

Dodge incoming fire, destroy enemy weapons, unleash special power-ups and survive the chaos as damaged ships fall from the sky, crash into the battlefield and explode around you.

Every level brings new enemies, new objectives and bigger threats — pushing your reactions, timing and firepower harder the further you go.

Easy to pick up. Hard to survive.

Defend the blocks. Destroy the enemy. Stay alive.
