Soccer Block Manager — Can You Take a Team of Blocks to the Top?

A new kind of football management game is coming to MasterGamer Adventures.

Meet Ted Wassoo — football manager, eternal optimist and the man tasked with taking an unlikely team of blocks from the lower divisions all the way to football glory.

Soccer Block Manager combines the strategy of a football management game with a live, visual block-based match engine where you can actually watch your decisions unfold on the pitch.

Build your squad, choose your formation, manage fitness and morale, select your captain, train your players and develop the tactical identity of your team.

Then comes match day.

Watch the game unfold live at 1×, 2× or 4× speed and step in when things aren't going according to plan. Change tactics, make substitutions and react to what the opposition is doing.

Create Your Own Prepared Plays

One of Soccer Block Manager's signature features is the Prepared Plays system.

Create and name your own routines, practise them during training and take up to three into each match.

The more your team practises a play, the better they become at executing it — but timing matters.

A routine called at exactly the right moment against an opponent vulnerable in that area could unlock the defence. Use the same trick too often, though, and the opposition may start anticipating it.

Football That Plays Out in Front of You

Matches aren't simply calculated behind a statistics screen.

The block players move, pass, defend, attack, switch play, create width and react to changing situations in real time.

Corners, free kicks, fouls, cards, penalties, goalkeeper saves and other match events all form part of the experience, supported by authentic football sound effects and crowd atmosphere.

And sometimes football simply refuses to follow the plan.

That's the point.

From the Bottom to the Top

Start in the lower divisions and try to build something special.

Develop the squad. Improve the club. Survive bad seasons. Chase promotion. Compete for championships and eventually take your team onto the biggest stage.

Every career develops differently — and every decision belongs to you.

Soccer Block Manager is coming soon to MasterGamer Adventures.

The blocks are ready.

Are you, Ted?
