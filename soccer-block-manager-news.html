Soccer Block Manager: The Ted Wasso Way — Prototype Coming Soon

A new MasterGamer management prototype is currently in development: Soccer Block Manager — The Ted Wasso Way.

Build your squad, appoint your captain, manage tactics, develop team chemistry and guide your block-style football club through the pressures of competition.

The prototype includes meaningful leadership, confidence, captaincy and Block Spirit systems, making every tactical decision and team-management choice matter.

An early browser-playable version is planned for release through the MasterGamer website in the coming weeks.

Watch this space for screenshots, development updates and the first opportunity to play Soccer Block Manager online.
