Three New MasterGamer Games Submitted to Apple and Google Play

Three new MasterGamer titles have now been submitted to the Apple App Store and Google Play:

  • Chicken Flapper II: Never Ending Journey
  • Zombie Block: Kill Everything
  • Wing Chun Chicken: Legend of Chun Du

All three games have been uploaded and are progressing through the app-store publishing and review processes. Subject to approval by Apple and Google, they should become available to download very soon.

Chicken Flapper II: Never Ending Journey launches players into a colourful arcade space adventure filled with enemy ships, power-ups, bosses, golden eggs and Hyperdrive bonus runs.

Zombie Block: Kill Everything delivers fast, block-style zombie survival action where players battle waves of undead enemies and fight to survive the chaos.

Wing Chun Chicken: Legend of Chun Du brings retro kung fu arcade action to mobile, featuring flying kicks, punches, KIAI power, enemies, dragons, helpers and boss battles across 100 levels.

Watch the MasterGamer website and social pages for the official download announcements and store links as soon as each title becomes available.
