Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Master Gamer collects, uses, stores, and protects information connected with its website, mobile games, browser games, support communications, and related services.

Information We May Collect

We may collect contact details you provide, support messages, device and technical information, gameplay diagnostics, platform identifiers, and analytics that help us improve performance and stability.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide support, maintain game services, understand crashes or bugs, improve releases, respond to privacy requests, and comply with legal obligations.

Third-Party Platforms

App Store, Google Play, analytics providers, video platforms, social networks, and hosting providers may process information under their own privacy terms when you use their services or follow external links.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Our games may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain information from your device when advertisements are requested, displayed or interacted with.

Depending on your device, location, privacy choices and applicable law, this information may include device identifiers, approximate location derived from information such as an IP address, advertising data, interactions with advertisements and the app, and diagnostic or performance information.

This information may be used to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and abuse, improve advertising services, and, where permitted and consented to, provide personalised advertising.

Tracking Permission on Apple Devices

On Apple devices, our games may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before allowing tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies.

You may choose Ask App Not to Track. Declining tracking permission does not prevent you from playing the game. Where advertising is available, you may still receive advertisements that are not based on cross-app tracking.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, we use Google's consent management tools to request and manage advertising privacy choices. Depending on your location, you may be presented with options relating to personalised advertising, advertising measurement and the use of your information by advertising partners.

Where available, these choices can be reviewed through the Privacy Choices option within the game.

Rewarded Advertisements

Some games may offer optional rewarded advertisements. For example, you may choose to watch an advertisement to continue after being defeated or to receive an optional in-game reward or power-up.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is voluntary and is not required to access normal game progression.

Third-Party Privacy Practices

Google processes information collected through AdMob according to Google's own privacy policies and advertising practices. We encourage users to review Google's privacy information to understand how Google processes data associated with its advertising services.

MasterGamer does not directly sell personal information to advertisers. Third-party advertising and analytics providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy practices, applicable law, and the privacy or consent choices made by the user.

Children

Master Gamer does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, email info@mastergamer.com.au.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or other assistance through the Data Request Page.

Contact

Email privacy enquiries to info@mastergamer.com.au.
