Master Gamer Application

AdPilot AI

Controlled advertising intelligence with human oversight.

AdPilot AI is an advertising monitoring and decision-support application developed as part of Master Gamer's application portfolio. It helps authorised users monitor advertising campaigns, collect performance evidence and prepare structured optimisation recommendations.

AdPilot AI connects to supported advertising platforms using authorised platform APIs. For Google Ads, users authorise access through Google OAuth without providing their Google password to AdPilot.

How AdPilot AI Works

Connect Securely

Authorised users connect supported advertising accounts using the platform's official authentication process.

Monitor

AdPilot collects authorised campaign configuration, status and performance information to monitor campaign delivery and build an evidence history.

Analyse

Campaign evidence may be analysed to identify changes in performance and prepare structured recommendations.

Human Control

AI-generated analysis does not independently control advertising spend. AdPilot is designed around human oversight and deterministic safety controls.

Google Ads Data Access

When an authorised user connects a Google Ads account, AdPilot AI may access Google Ads account and campaign information made available through the permissions granted by that user. This can include account and campaign identifiers, campaign configuration and status, budgets, advertising and creative metadata, and performance information such as impressions, clicks, conversions, conversion value and spend.

This information is used to provide AdPilot's campaign monitoring, evidence, analysis, reporting and recommendation features.

Current Google Ads Access

AdPilot's current Google Ads integration is used for read-only monitoring and evidence collection. It does not automatically change Google Ads campaigns.

Privacy and Control

Users remain in control of their connected accounts and may revoke AdPilot's Google access through their Google Account permissions. More information about how AdPilot accesses, uses, stores and protects Google data is provided in the Master Gamer Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Access

AdPilot AI is currently in controlled rollout. Application access is limited to authorised users.
